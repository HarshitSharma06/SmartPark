Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the AI Parking Predictor work?
SmartPark uses an on-device rule-based algorithm that combines real-time parking availability with historical time-of-day occupancy trends, ETA, and distance to estimate your chance % of finding a spot.
Q: How do I navigate to a parking lot?
Tap on any parking marker or item in the list to open details, then tap the "Navigate" button. SmartPark will open directions directly in Google Maps.
Q: What is the What-If simulation?
What-If lets you simulate future arrival times, filter by price or maximum walking distance, and discover alternative parking options before you start driving.
Q: Can I use SmartPark offline?
Yes! When offline or in Airplane Mode, SmartPark displays cached parking data and allows you to view your saved favourites.
Q: How do I export my parking history?
Go to the "Saved" tab, select "History", and tap "Export CSV". You can share your history file or import it on another device.
